1. Cool fact — How do chemists pull medicine from a plant?

When chemists want medicine from a plant, they do not just boil the leaves and hope. First they dry and crush the plant. Then they add a solvent — a liquid that can dissolve the useful chemical. The mixture is filtered to remove the leftover plant bits. Next the chemist may cool or evaporate the liquid until tiny crystals appear. Those crystals are often the pure compound. This careful process is called extraction. It turns a green plant into a clear, tested medicine that doctors can measure and trust.

Key words: plant · solvent · dissolve · filter · crystal · extract
