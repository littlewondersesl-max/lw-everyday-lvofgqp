2. Cool fact — Why does metal rust?

Iron is a metal we use in bikes, nails, and bridges. When iron touches water and oxygen in the air, a slow chemical reaction begins. Tiny bits of iron join with oxygen and make a new substance: iron oxide — the reddish-brown flakes we call rust. Rust is weaker than clean iron, so a rusty bike can break more easily. Salt water makes rust form even faster. Paint, oil, or a plastic coat can keep water and air away — and that slows rust down.
