Jack and the Beanstalk

Easy English (short lines)

JACK AND THE BEANSTALK
Easy English for young readers
A new telling from the old public-domain tale

Page 1
Jack lived with his mother.
They were poor.
They had one cow.
Her name was Milky-White.

Page 2
Every day the cow gave milk.
Jack sold the milk.
That was how they got food.

Page 3
One morning the cow gave no milk.
"What can we do?" said Mother.
"I will sell the cow," said Jack.

Page 4
Jack took the cow to town.
On the road he met an old man.
"Good morning, Jack," said the old man.
"How do you know my name?" said Jack.

Page 5
"I know many things," said the old man.
"I will give you these beans for your cow.
They are magic beans.
Plant them at night.
In the morning they will grow up to the sky."

Page 6
Jack looked at the beans.
They were red and green and gold.
"Yes," said Jack.
He gave the man the cow.
He put the beans in his pocket.

Page 7
Jack ran home.
"Did you sell the cow?" said Mother.
"Yes!" said Jack. "I got these magic beans!"
Mother was not happy.
"Beans? We need money, Jack. Not beans!"
She threw the beans out of the window.
"Go to bed," she said.
Jack went to bed with no supper.

Page 8
In the morning, Jack woke up.
His room was dark.
He looked out of the window.
A giant beanstalk was there!
It went up and up and up.
It went into the clouds.

Page 9
Jack opened the window.
He jumped onto the beanstalk.
It was like a tall green ladder.
Jack climbed and climbed and climbed.
He climbed up to the sky.

Page 10
At the top there was a long road.
Jack walked and walked.
He came to a big, big house.
A tall woman stood at the door.

Page 11
"Please, I am hungry," said Jack.
"Go away," said the woman.
"My husband is a giant.
He likes to eat boys."
"Please," said Jack. "I have no food."
The woman was kind.
She gave Jack bread and milk.

Page 12
THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.
The house shook.
"Hide!" said the woman.
Jack hid in a big pot.
The giant came in.

Page 13
The giant was huge.
He sniffed the air.
"Fee-fi-fo-fum!
I smell a boy.
I smell a little one!"
"No, no," said his wife.
"You smell your breakfast.
Sit down and eat."

Page 14
The giant ate and ate.
Then he took out a bag of gold.
He counted the gold.
His eyes closed.
He began to snore.
The house shook again.

Page 15
Jack came out of the pot.
He was very quiet.
He took the bag of gold.
He ran to the beanstalk.
He climbed down, down, down.

Page 16
"Mother! Look!" said Jack.
He showed her the gold.
"The beans were magic!"
They bought food.
They were happy for a while.
Then the gold was gone.

Page 17
Jack climbed the beanstalk again.
He went to the big house.
He hid in the pot.
THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.
"Fee-fi-fo-fum!" said the giant.
"I smell a boy!"
"Eat your breakfast," said his wife.

Page 18
After breakfast the giant said,
"Bring me my hen."
The hen was brown and fat.
"Lay!" said the giant.
The hen laid an egg.
The egg was gold!

Page 19
The giant fell asleep.
Jack took the hen.
The hen cried, "Cluck! Cluck!"
The giant woke up.
"My hen! My hen!"
Jack ran.
He climbed down the beanstalk fast.

Page 20
Now Jack and his mother had a magic hen.
Every day Jack said, "Lay!"
Every day the hen laid a gold egg.
But Jack wanted to see the sky one more time.

Page 21
Jack climbed the beanstalk again.
This time he hid in a big box.
THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.
"Fee-fi-fo-fum!
I smell a boy!
I know I do!"
The giant looked in the pot.
Jack was not there.

Page 22
"Bring me my harp," said the giant.
The harp was gold.
"Sing!" said the giant.
The harp sang a sweet song.
The giant smiled.
Soon he was asleep.

Page 23
Jack took the harp.
The harp called out, "Help! Help!"
The giant opened his eyes.
"Stop, boy!"
Jack ran and ran.
The giant ran after him.

Page 24
Jack got to the beanstalk.
He climbed down fast.
The giant climbed down too.
The beanstalk shook.
"Mother! Bring the axe!" said Jack.

Page 25
Mother ran with the axe.
Jack chopped the beanstalk.
Chop!
Chop!
The beanstalk fell.
The giant fell down, down, down.
He did not come back.

Page 26
Jack had the gold harp.
Jack had the magic hen.
Jack had his mother.
They were not poor now.
They were safe.
They were happy.

The End

Words to learn
poor, sell, magic, plant, climb, giant, hide, gold, hen, egg, harp, axe, safe, happy
