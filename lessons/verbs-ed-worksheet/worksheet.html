Tips

Add -ing for now.
play → playing
Add -ed for the past.
play → played
Ends in e: drop the e for -ing, and add -d.
create → creating → created
Short CVC word: double the last letter.
stop → stopping → stopped
Consonant + y: keep the y for -ing, but change y to i for -ed: cry → crying → cried
