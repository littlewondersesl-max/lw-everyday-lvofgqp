Teacher Answer Key

Word Maths, grade nine  ·  Not for students  ·  All answers in words

One. twenty-seven litres
five equal parts; forty-five divided by five is nine; red is three parts, so nine times three
Two. four hundred and fifty grams
twelve is one and a half times eight; three hundred times one and a half
Three. two thousand seven hundred and sixty rand
fifteen percent of two thousand four hundred is three hundred and sixty; add this to the original price
Four. five hundred and seventy-six rand
twenty percent of eight hundred is one hundred and sixty, leaving six hundred and forty; ten percent of six hundred and forty is sixty-four; six hundred and forty minus sixty-four. (This is not the same as thirty percent off the original price.)
Five. six hundred rand
simple interest: five thousand times six times two, then divide by one hundred
Six. seven and a half hours
three hundred and sixty divided by forty-eight
Seven. seventy-seven
sixty-eight plus seventy-five plus eighty-one plus eighty-four is three hundred and eight; divide by four
Eight. eleven
a number times three, plus seven, equals forty; forty minus seven is thirty-three; thirty-three divided by three
Nine. Perimeter: forty-six metres
two times fifteen plus eight
Area: one hundred and twenty square metres
fifteen times eight
Ten. thirty square centimetres
one half times ten times six
Eleven. one hundred and forty-four square centimetres
twelve squared, or twelve times twelve
Twelve. fourteen metres
the square root of one hundred and ninety-six is fourteen, because fourteen times fourteen is one hundred and ninety-six
Thirteen. minus five degrees Celsius
three minus eight. Also accept five degrees below zero.
Fourteen. forty-four rand
three times twelve is thirty-six; four times five is twenty; the total is fifty-six; one hundred minus fifty-six
Fifteen. fifteen, sixteen and seventeen
three consecutive numbers sum to forty-eight, so three times the middle number is forty-eight; the middle number is sixteen

Write the equation in words, then find the number

A. Equation: a number multiplied by four, minus five, equals twenty-seven
The number: eight (twenty-seven plus five is thirty-two; thirty-two divided by four)
B. Equation: a number multiplied by five, plus nine, equals forty-four
The number: seven (forty-four minus nine is thirty-five; thirty-five divided by five)
C. Equation: a number multiplied by two, plus eleven, equals thirty-five
The number: twelve (thirty-five minus eleven is twenty-four; twenty-four divided by two)
D. Equation: a number divided by three, plus six, equals thirteen
The number: twenty-one (thirteen minus six is seven; seven times three)
