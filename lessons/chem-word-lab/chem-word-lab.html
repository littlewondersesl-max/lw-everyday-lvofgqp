2. Cool fact — Why do onions make you cry?

When you cut an onion, you break tiny cells inside it. The onion releases a chemical. That chemical mixes with the air and turns into a gas. The gas floats up to your eyes. Your eyes think something dangerous is there, so they make tears to wash the gas away. The tears are mostly water, but they also have a little salt and protein. Scientists call the gas a kind of acid vapor. If you chill the onion first, the chemical moves more slowly — so you cry less!

Underlined / key words: cell · chemical · gas · vapor · acid · tear
