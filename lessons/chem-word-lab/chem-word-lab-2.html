2. Cool fact — Why do fireflies glow?

Fireflies make their own light. Inside their bodies, a protein called luciferin mixes with oxygen. An enzyme helps the reaction start. Energy is released as soft yellow-green light — almost no heat. Scientists call this bioluminescence (life + light). The firefly uses the glow to find a partner at night. Some animals use the same trick to scare predators or to hunt. Humans copy this idea in glow sticks and in lab tests that look for tiny amounts of life.

Key words: glow · oxygen · enzyme · reaction · energy · light
