1. Cool fact — Why do fireflies glow?

Fireflies make their own light. Inside their bodies, a chemical called luciferin mixes with oxygen. A protein called an enzyme helps the reaction start. Energy is released as soft yellow-green light — almost no heat. Scientists call this bioluminescence (life + light). The firefly uses the glow to find a partner at night. Some animals use the same trick to scare predators or to hunt. Humans use a similar idea in glow sticks and in lab tests that look for tiny amounts of life.

Key words: glow · oxygen · enzyme · reaction · energy · light
