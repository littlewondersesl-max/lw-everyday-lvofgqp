# Word Lab: reorder + fact-check log (19 Nov 2026)

## What changed everywhere
- **Order is now 1 Cool fact, 2 Brain teasers, 3 worksheet activity, 4 Speak** on all 50 Word Lab sheets (student PDF, answer-key PDF and the PNG preview) and on the 4 Chemistry Word Lab pages (`chem-word-lab`, `-2`, `-3`, `-4`, their downloads, and the book-viewer page images).
- **Word hunt is now rare.** It stays on 5 of the 50 sheets (the Atoms and elements, Solar System, Animal adaptations, Deserts and Vikings sheets, 1 in 10, spread out). The other 45 use one of six other activity types, and no two neighbouring sheets use the same type. All 4 Chemistry Word Lab pages had their hunt replaced.
- Hub cards and detail pages show the new order and the activity type; the EN / EN+中文 toggle translates the new labels.

## Checked and left unchanged
All 50 cool facts and all 150 brain teasers were read for accuracy, ambiguity and whether they follow from the fact. Numbers checked: 118 elements, 206 bones, 71 percent water, Everest 8,849 m, Nile about 6,650 km, light 300,000 km/s, Olympics 776 BCE, Apollo 11 dates (16 and 20 July 1969), Gutenberg about 1440, Roman Empire at its largest around 117 CE, the Western empire ending in 476 CE, Vikings about 800 to 1100 CE. All fine.
