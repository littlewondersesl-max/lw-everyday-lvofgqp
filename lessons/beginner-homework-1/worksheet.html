Teacher Answer Key

Beginner English Homework 1  ·  Not for students

1. Bicycle

Students trace and write the word bicycle. Then they colour the bike.

Labels, around the bike:

top left (on the saddle) → seat

top right (small circle on the bar) → bell

middle right (the bar the rider holds) → handlebar

left (the back tyre) → wheel

2. Light and dark colours

In each pair, circle the dark colour:

circle dark blue  ·  circle dark green  ·  circle dark red

light red is pink.

Accept any clearly lighter/darker pair of the same colour family.

3. How old are you?

Trace and free-writing practice. The “Write about you” line is the child’s own age.

Cakes, left to right:

5 candles → I am 5 years old.

6 candles → I am 6 years old.

7 candles → I am 7 years old.

4. China or Chinese?

China = the country / a place.   Chinese = the people or the language.

  1. I am from China.
  2. I am Chinese.
  3. I speak Chinese.
