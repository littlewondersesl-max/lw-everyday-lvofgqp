Tips

Add -er to compare two things.
tall → taller
Add -est to compare three or more.
tall → tallest
Double the last letter:
big → bigger → biggest
Change y to i:
happy → happier → happiest
If the word ends in e, just add -r or -st: nice → nicer → nicest
